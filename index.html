    How it works

        Questions

        About textile costing in Texel

        What is Texel?

        Texel is textile management software for weaving mills and fabric manufacturers. It handles yarn costing, fabric cost sheets, warp and weft design sheets, buyer records and Excel or PDF reports in one place.

        How does Texel calculate fabric cost?

        You enter the width, reed, pick, ply, yarn counts, yarn rates and dyeing cost. Texel calculates the warp and weft weight and cost, then adds weaving, washing, mending, twisting, transport, profit and 5% GST.

        Can Texel give a colour-wise design splitup?

        Yes. Enter the warp and weft colour order with thread counts, and Texel repeats it across the width. It then shows the threads and yarn weight for each colour, per metre and for the whole order.

        What happens to old quotes when yarn prices change?

        Each costing keeps the yarn prices it was saved with. When rates change, Texel flags the outdated costing. Recalculating creates a new version and keeps the earlier quote in its history.

        Can I export costing sheets to Excel or PDF?

        Yes. Costing sheets download as Excel or as a PDF with the design image, and you can choose which versions to include.

        Does Texel include textile inventory management?

        Not yet. Yarn and fabric inventory, order tracking and GST invoicing are coming soon. Costing, design sheets and buyer management are available today.
