    How it works

    One thread from enquiry to quote

    1. 1

      Take the request

      Log what the buyer asked for, with a fabric photo and colour. It waits in Pending until someone costs it.

    2. 2

      Build the costing

      Enter width, reed, pick, ply and yarn counts. Weights, costs, profit and GST come back calculated.

    3. 3

      Add options

      Price other constructions or colours as options under the same costing, so they stay together.

    4. 4

      Send and export

      Download the sheet as Excel or a PDF with the design image, and mark the costing as Sent.

    5. 5

      Reprice when yarn moves

      Texel flags costings with outdated prices. Recalculate, and the old quote stays on record.

    What's inside

    Built around how a mill prices cloth

    The formulas run on the server, so everyone in the office gets the same answer. If a yarn count has no weight on file, Texel stops and tells you instead of pricing it at zero.

    Yarn price book

    One list of counts, hank weights and rates. Each change is logged, and the dashboard charts a yarn's price over time.
